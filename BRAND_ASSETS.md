# Mothership brand assets

## Brand hierarchy

- **UME** is the parent/master brand.
- The **Original Whale Mark** is the Mothership product symbol.
- **MOTHERSHIP** is the product wordmark.
- **Bounded Action Authority** is the product descriptor.

The whale remains calm, protective, and precise. It is not a generic container-whale mark, and it does not represent
an executor, model runtime, or grant of autonomous authority.

## Provenance

The Original Whale Mark is based on an original drawing by the creator's son. The banner and logo preserve that drawing
as the source of the product symbol rather than replacing it with an unrelated corporate mark.
